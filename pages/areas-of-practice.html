Integrated accounting, tax, payroll, and compliance solutions for foreign companies and investors operating in Costa Rica.

At COLBS Estudio Legal, we understand that establishing and operating a business in a foreign jurisdiction requires more than legal representation. Companies need reliable accounting, accurate financial reporting, timely tax filings, and ongoing compliance with local regulations.

Our Accounting & Tax Compliance practice provides comprehensive support to foreign-owned companies, multinational businesses, investors, and local subsidiaries operating in Costa Rica.

Working alongside our corporate and legal teams, our accounting professionals help clients navigate Costa Rica’s financial, tax, and administrative requirements, allowing them to focus on growing their businesses while maintaining compliance with local obligations.

Our Services
  • Accounting & Bookkeeping: Monthly accounting, financial records, reconciliations, and preparation of financial statements.
  • Tax Compliance & Filings: Preparation and submission of monthly and annual tax returns, including VAT, corporate income tax, withholding taxes, and other applicable declarations.
  • Payroll Administration: Payroll processing, salary calculations, statutory deductions, social security contributions, and employment-related reporting.
  • Corporate & Tax Registrations: Registration and updates with the Costa Rican Tax Administration, Social Security Fund (CCSS), National Insurance Institute (INS), and other relevant authorities.
  • Corporate Compliance: Monitoring and coordination of recurring corporate, tax, and administrative obligations, including applicable beneficial ownership reporting.
  • Financial Reporting: Preparation of financial information and management reports for foreign shareholders, parent companies, and international management teams.
  • Business Setup Support: Accounting and tax onboarding for newly incorporated companies, foreign subsidiaries, and businesses establishing operations in Costa Rica.
